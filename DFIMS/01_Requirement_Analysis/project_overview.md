**Role:** System Analyst & Designer

**Industry/Domain**: Inventory/Agroindustri  
**Focus:** Demand Forecasting System & Inventory Management(DFIMS)

---

# 📊**Business Background**
UD Mitra Tani is a company focused on the management and sale of agricultural supplies. Currently, its business processes are still handled manually:  
•	Recording, transactions, and inventory management are all performed manually using traditional bookkeeping.  
•	As a result, information regarding stock levels cannot be obtained quickly or accurately.  
•	Furthermore, the company faces issues with stockouts, as it lacks an adequate method for forecasting inventory requirements for future periods.  
The proposed solution aims to enable the monitoring of incoming and outgoing goods, automate the calculation of stock movements, improve efficiency 
and accuracy in procurement transactions, and provide forecasts for future inventory needs.

---

# 🎯**Objectives**
1.	Improve the efficiency of recording incoming and outgoing product transactions  
2.	Optimize the control of inventory stock data  
3.	Provide real-time data access for monitoring business performance  
4.	Forecast future inventory requirements
